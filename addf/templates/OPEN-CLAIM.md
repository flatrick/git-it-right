## Open Claim invariants

-   One coherent, falsifiable proposition per file — the same splitting
    rule as any other Claim in the framework
    (`skills/evidence-and-verification.md`'s "split a Claim" guidance).
-   Follows the same State/Basis model as every other Claim: `UNVERIFIED`
    until a Verification settles it; Basis names the applicable
    Verification, never a green result, authority statement, or accepted
    risk alone.
-   Carries no Task-lifecycle State of its own — only the Claim State
    model above. A standing Claim is current retrieval, not archived
    history, for as long as its resolution remains decision-relevant.
-   Not itself a promotion target: once `VERIFIED`, it flows through
    Stewardship's normal Knowledge-promotion step into `knowledge/`
    (`templates/KNOWLEDGE.md`) like any other Claim; it does not become
    Knowledge merely by living here. Once settled with no further tracking
    value (`REFUTED`, or superseded), retire it under Stewardship's normal
    archive rules.
-   Created or refreshed only through Stewardship's "Carry forward open
    Claims" step, at a Task's own terminalization
    (`skills/stewardship.md`), for a Claim whose resolution remains
    decision-relevant beyond that Task.
-   Listed in `INDEX.md`'s `## Open Claims` category in the same pass it
    is created, refreshed, or retired.
