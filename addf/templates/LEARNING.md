## Promotion check

Do not automatically turn an incident into permanent guidance.

Ask:

1.  Is the learning reusable?
2.  Is it sufficiently stable?
3.  Can tooling enforce it instead of prose?
4.  Is it procedural (Skill), normative (Rule), factual (Knowledge), or
    deterministic capability (Command)?
5.  Does its behavioral value justify its context and maintenance cost?
